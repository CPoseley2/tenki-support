About Tenki.

ONE SENTENCE

Tenki turns your iPhone or iPad into a tactile wireless numpad, trackpad, and keyboard for your Mac.

SHORT DESCRIPTION

MacBook keyboards leave out the numpad. Tenki puts one on the iPhone or iPad already beside you, with classic and spreadsheet-first layouts, haptic feedback, selectable key sounds, a full trackpad, and remote keyboard. It connects directly over the local network without an account, ads, analytics, tracking, or cloud service.

KEY DIFFERENTIATOR

Tenki starts with a focused spreadsheet-input problem instead of generic remote control, then adds trackpad and keyboard surfaces without introducing a subscription or cloud account.
