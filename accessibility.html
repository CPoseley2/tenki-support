ACCESSIBILITY

Clear controls.
More ways to use them.

Tenki is built around large, focused control surfaces, crisp visual boundaries, restrained blur, and feedback that does not depend on color alone.

01

Dark-first interfaces

Receiver, Console, Terminal, and Vector keep Tenki’s common controls on dark surfaces. The Clean theme adapts to your system appearance, so you can choose the environment that feels most comfortable.

02

More than color

Connection states combine color with text and recognizable symbols. Selected modes use icons, placement, shape, and contrast—so color is never the only signal carrying important information.

03

Full-screen controls

The trackpad and keypad can become spacious, focused surfaces. Large targets and simplified layouts reduce visual clutter and make the primary action easier to find and use.

04

Crisp, low-blur surfaces

Core controls favor defined edges, clear grouping, and restrained translucency. Decorative effects stay behind the work surface so buttons, keys, and status information remain distinct.

05

Optional sensory feedback

Key sounds and haptics can reinforce an action without replacing its visual response. You can adjust or disable feedback to match your preferences and surroundings.

06

Familiar platform controls

Tenki uses native buttons, pickers, toggles, sliders, and the system keyboard wherever practical, preserving familiar interaction patterns across iPhone and iPad.

WORK IN PROGRESS

Accessibility is a continuing practice.

We are continuing to evaluate VoiceOver and Voice Control behavior for Tenki’s custom trackpad gestures, larger text across compact layouts, reduced-motion behavior, and contrast across every theme.

We would rather be clear about what is still improving than overstate support. Each release is an opportunity to make the app easier to perceive, understand, and operate.
