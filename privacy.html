PRIVACY POLICY

Local by design.
Private by default.

Effective July 29, 2026

Overview

Tenki turns an iPhone into a trackpad, keypad, and keyboard for a Mac running the TenkiMac companion. Tenki is designed to work locally between your devices and does not require an account.

Information we collect

Tenki does not collect personal information. The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, cloud storage, or third-party data collection services.

Local network communication

Tenki uses Apple’s local networking technologies to discover nearby Macs running TenkiMac and to send the input commands you request. Device names and commands are exchanged only as needed to establish and use that local connection. Tenki does not send this information to a developer-operated server.

Permissions

Local Network on iPhone

Tenki requests Local Network access so it can discover and connect to TenkiMac. Without this permission, the app cannot find a Mac receiver.

Accessibility on Mac

TenkiMac requires macOS Accessibility permission to deliver the keyboard, text, pointer, click, and scroll actions you request to the active Mac app. TenkiMac does not use Accessibility permission to inspect private content, read documents, monitor browsing, or collect data.

Data retention

Because Tenki does not collect data on developer-operated servers, there is no server-side personal data to retain or delete. Preferences such as theme and control settings are stored locally on your device and can be removed by deleting the app.

Children’s privacy

Tenki does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

If Tenki’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Tenki is developed and supported by Collin Poseley. Questions about privacy or the app can be submitted through the official support form.
